## B1: commit to client latency

Table `bench` with 1000 rows, 10 clients, one writer at 100 txn/s for 30 s (ran 30.0 s). Each txn updates one random row; `sent_us` is taken right before the `BEGIN; UPDATE; COMMIT` round trip. A client's latency for a row is measured when the `commit` event that carries the row arrives.

| measure | p50 ms | p95 ms | p99 ms | max ms |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| commit sent → commit event received | 2.37 | 4.46 | 7.72 | 21.64 |
| writer COMMIT round trip | 1.50 | 2.90 | 5.79 | 17.29 |

Updates written: 3000. Updates received per client: [3000, 3000, 3000, 3000, 3000, 3000, 3000, 3000, 3000, 3000] (missed in total: 0). Latency samples: 30000.

## B3: catch-up cost

Bytes are the SSE bytes consumed up to and including that event. A full catch-up was run once beforehand and discarded (warm-up). The change set is one transaction: 70 updates, 20 deletes, 10 inserts (100 rows). Rows events are parsed after the clock stops.
